# Original weapon synthesis

Runtime audio is generated by `tools/synthesize_audio.py` from mathematical
oscillators, filtered seeded noise, layered transients, and short room reflections.
No downloaded recordings are sampled by the current runtime effects.

- Bolter: pressure crack, low-mid body, delayed breech clack; four variations.
- Grenade: fast detonation, bass pressure, rubble and air tail; three variations.
- Relay collapse: descending electrical suction, metal collapse and debris; two variations.
- Chainsword: short dry rev, irregular low motor pulses and a noisy tooth rasp;
  three variations, without the previous pitched buzz or reflection tail.

Regenerate with Python and numpy:

```sh
python3 tools/synthesize_audio.py
```

Outputs are 48 kHz stereo PCM WAVs in `godot/assets/audio`. The reproducible
measurements are in `synthesis.json`; `build/audio/weapon-preview.wav` provides
one sample of each sound family with gaps. Runtime gain leaves mix headroom,
with a master limiter and separate gun/effect voice pools.

The legacy CC0 recordings remain archived in `source/`; their download provenance
is in `manifest.json`. They are no longer used by the runtime sound effects.
