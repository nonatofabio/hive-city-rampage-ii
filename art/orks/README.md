All figures face screen RIGHT in three-quarter isometric view, chunky detailed gothic pixel art matching a 1990s isometric shooter. Four columns are four coherent walking-cycle poses: left foot forward, passing, right foot forward, passing. Row 2: Ork Shoota, green tusked face, red bandana, scrap-metal armor, large crude machine gun aimed to the right; all four frames same gun scale. Row 3: imposing Ork Warboss with massive armored shoulders, checkered black-white panels, red armor plates, power claw and huge gun, roughly 30 percent bulkier within cell. All twelve figures match their row's identity and equipment exactly. No humans, no space marines. Crisp silhouettes, visible green faces, original illustrated assets, no cropped limbs.
