# Weapon and destruction audio

The current bolter, grenade, relay-collapse, and chainsword WAVs are original
procedural sound designs generated by `tools/synthesize_audio.py`: seeded noise,
oscillators, filters, metallic partials, envelopes, and room reflections. They
contain no sampled commercial-game audio or downloaded recordings.

The former CC0 source clips by Kenney (Sci-Fi Sounds and Impact Sounds) remain
archived under `art/audio/source/`, with their original notices and provenance.
Those recordings are not used in the current runtime WAVs.
