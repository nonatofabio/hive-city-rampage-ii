# Gunfire audio credits

Only the four approved bolter reports are used by the game. Music and all other sound effects have been removed.

The unchanged mixes `bolter_0.wav` through `bolter_3.wav` use three CC0 source clips, retained in `art/audio/source/` in the source repository:

| Source | Author | License | Used clips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | `explosionCrunch_000.ogg` |
| [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | `impactMetal_heavy_000.ogg`, `impactMetal_light_001.ogg` |

The packs' original license files are included. CC0 permits commercial reuse without royalties. Source archive URLs and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `art/audio/manifest.json` in the source repository.

The original project’s audio generator layered a brief explosion transient, metallic body, delayed mechanical action, and original sub-bass/noise. The original synthesis portions and mixes are also dedicated to [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). These are original heavy-gun designs, not sounds extracted from a commercial game.
