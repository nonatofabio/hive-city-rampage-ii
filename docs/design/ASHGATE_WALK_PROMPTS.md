First attempt (rejected: seven columns and inconsistent alternation):

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game sprite atlas: draw a true eight-frame heavy armored WALK CYCLE of ONLY the LOWER BODY (waist belt, pelvis, two thighs, knee armor, shins, boots) matching the blue and gold armored soldier in the reference images. No upper body, arms, gun, head, scenery, shadows, text or grid lines. Transparent alpha background. Exactly 8 columns by 5 rows in equally sized cells, total 40 distinct lower-body poses, landscape 1536x1024. Each cell has identical scale and pelvis position at its horizontal center, waist near the top, boots near bottom, generous clear margins. ROW 1 walking screen RIGHT, side view. ROW 2 walking upper RIGHT, rear three-quarter view. ROW 3 walking UP, rear view. ROW 4 walking lower RIGHT, front three-quarter view. ROW 5 walking DOWN, front view. Each row is chronological eight-frame looping walk: 1 left heel reaches forward/right toe back (contact); 2 weight compresses onto left foot, right heel lifts (down); 3 right boot lifted with clearly bent knee passing left planted leg (passing); 4 right thigh swings forward, left heel pushes off (up); 5 opposite right-heel contact; 6 weight on right, left heel lifts; 7 left bent knee and raised boot passing planted right; 8 left leg reaches forward for next contact. Feet must alternate supporting the body and lifting clear off ground, not a wide stationary stance. Strong readable silhouettes, visible sole in lifted phases, 60-degree knee articulation in passing poses. Maintain waist center and identical armor design across all poses. Compact clean sprite aesthetic, flat cobalt panels, dark outlines, small gold knee skulls and gold bands, black ribbed joint undersuit. Preserve reference palette and chunky boot proportions. No white fringe, no lighting glow. Every pose must be completely separate in its own equal grid cell.
